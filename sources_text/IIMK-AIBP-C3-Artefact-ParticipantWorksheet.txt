 WHAT THIS ASSIGNMENT IS
 An organisation- or function-level decision artefact. You will assess AI readiness across six dimensions, build and sequence a
 5-7 opportunity portfolio, examine the competitive AI landscape, and redesign one affected role around human-AI
 complementarity.



 CONTINUITY FROM COURSES 1 AND 2
 You may reuse the same organisational context and one or more opportunities from earlier assignments, but you do not
 have to. Course 3 is broader: it asks you to move from a single use-case decision to a portfolio and organisational-readiness
 perspective. Reuse is acceptable only where the opportunity still fits the purpose of this assessment.

How to Complete This Assignment
The assessment tests organisational judgement, portfolio thinking and workforce redesign - not technical
implementation.
       Choose one clear assessment scope and keep it consistent: whole organisation, business unit/division, function, or a clearly
        labelled representative/anonymised context.
       Use High / Medium / Low maturity ratings only after considering the sub-dimensions and available evidence. Do not treat
        self-perception as evidence.
       Build 5-7 credible AI opportunities. They may include traditional AI/ML, GenAI, multimodal or agentic AI where
        appropriate; do not force every opportunity into GenAI.
       For competitive analysis, use public, current and identifiable sources. If evidence is unavailable, write Not evidenced
        rather than inventing a signal.
       Do not disclose confidential, personal, proprietary or commercially sensitive information. Anonymisation and clearly
        labelled assumptions are acceptable.
       Keep the workforce analysis focused on tasks and role redesign. The assignment does not ask you to make or justify
        headcount-reduction decisions.
       Make the final portfolio internally consistent with the maturity constraints you establish in Part A.

    CONFIDENTIALITY, ASSUMPTIONS AND HYPOTHETICAL CONTEXTS
    You may use a real context, an anonymised real context, a representative/composite professional context, or a clearly
    labelled hypothetical context where real information cannot be safely disclosed. Estimates and assumptions are acceptable
    when their basis is stated. Do not invent organisational facts and present them as real.



    IMPORTANT: ASSESSMENT SCOPE
    You are not required to assess your entire corporation if you do not have sufficient visibility. A business unit or function that
    you understand well is acceptable. Your claims should match the scope you can credibly assess.



What This Assignment Is Not
       A full enterprise AI strategy or governance operating model.
       An investment-grade ROI, NPV or payback business case.
       A build-vs-buy-vs-partner or vendor-selection exercise.
       A legal/regulatory opinion.
       A production implementation plan.
       A headcount-reduction plan.
       A requirement to use the most advanced AI technology available.
