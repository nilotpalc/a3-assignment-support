    HOW TO USE THIS RUBRIC
    Use this alongside the Assignment 3 worksheet. The descriptors show what strong performance looks like; they are not a second set of
    tasks. Exact marks within a criterion depend on completeness, evidence, reasoning and consistency. The assessment rewards
    defensible organisational judgement, not a predetermined maturity rating, portfolio type, competitive position or workforce outcome.

    FINAL SCORE RULE
    Final recorded score = content score + quality modifier, capped at 100 and floored at 0. The modifier is document-level and does not
    re-score individual criterion weaknesses.




What the assessment rewards
       Scope discipline: claims stay within what you can credibly observe or evidence.
       Evidence-based maturity judgements rather than optimistic self-rating.
       A portfolio that connects opportunities to readiness constraints, time-to-impact, risk and strategic significance.
       Competitive conclusions that distinguish public signals from assumptions and “not evidenced” gaps.
       Workforce redesign at task level: automation frees capacity for augmentation and irreducibly human work rather than
        assuming headcount reduction.
       Cross-section coherence: maturity, opportunity sequencing, competitive position and workforce redesign should tell one
        decision story.

What does not earn extra marks
       Assessing the whole corporation when you only have evidence for one function or business unit.
       Rating the organisation “High” on maturity without evidence.
       Including more than seven opportunities or choosing only the most technologically advanced opportunities.
       Labelling every priority a Quick Win or every initiative “Lead”.
       Using all five competitive-intelligence source types when some are genuinely unavailable; “Not evidenced / Not applicable”
        is acceptable when handled transparently.
       Decomposing 30-50 tasks in the main submission. The assessed requirement is 15-20 representative tasks; a fuller
        decomposition is optional.
       Proposing headcount reduction as proof of successful workforce redesign.

Quality Modifier (+5 to -5)
    Modifier                                                               Participant-facing descriptor

    +5                                                                     Exceptional executive artefact: highly specific, coherent, evidence-
                                                                           disciplined, concise and genuinely decision-ready.

    +3 to +4                                                               Strong professional artefact with very good synthesis, evidence
                                                                           use and cross-section consistency; only minor weaknesses.

    +1 to +2                                                               Above-standard document-level synthesis or usability, though not
                                                                           consistently exceptional.

    0                                                                      Competent submission meeting normal professional expectations.

    -1 to -2                                                               Noticeable document-level weaknesses: avoidable inconsistency,
                                                                           weak synthesis/source discipline, or presentation problems.

    -3 to -4                                                               Significant document-level problems: repeated unsupported
                                                                           assertions, major contradictions, or serious non-compliance.

    -5                                                                     Material integrity/submission-quality failure: undisclosed
                                                                           fabrication, extensive placeholders, missing declaration, unusable
                                                                           structure or equivalent fundamental problem.



    NO DOUBLE PENALTY
    The modifier does not deduct the same weakness twice. If a criterion already loses marks for weak evidence or reasoning, the
    modifier changes only when that weakness becomes a document-wide coherence, integrity or professional-quality problem.




Quick Pre-Submission Self-Check
        [ ] My assessment scope matches what I can genuinely observe or evidence.
        [ ] Every maturity rating cites indicators and considers the taught sub-dimensions.
        [ ] My 5-7 opportunities are distinct business problems, not a list of AI technologies.
        [ ] My Quick Win / Strategic Bet / Moonshot labels are consistent with the maturity evidence.
        [ ] My top three include explicit readiness conditions and circumstances that would change the priority.
        [ ] Material competitor/peer claims are traceable to current sources; unavailable evidence is marked honestly.
        [ ] My Lead / Fast follow / Wait decisions are initiative-specific.
        [ ] I completed 15-20 discrete role tasks, not broad activities.
        [ ] My role redesign explains where released capacity goes and how success measures change.
        [ ] My final workforce test allows the possibility that Human alone, AI alone or Human+AI may perform best for different
         tasks.
        [ ] Assumptions, anonymisation, hypothetical elements and AI assistance are clearly disclosed.

    THE STANDARD TO AIM FOR
    A strong Course 3 artefact connects four decisions: where the organisation is ready, which opportunities deserve attention, how AI is
    changing the competitive context, and how work should be redesigned around human-AI complementarity.
