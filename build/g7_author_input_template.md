## D1. Select the workforce focus - 3 marks

**Mandatory:** select one role affected by a top-three opportunity. Prefer Priority 1; if choosing another top-three opportunity, explain its clearer workforce implications.

**Budget:** 200 words maximum across the final D1 response.

**Layout recommendation:** labelled paragraphs, retaining the following worksheet field labels.

**Selected opportunity**

P4 - Sector-matched value cases

**Role / role family affected**

Sales Account Manager

**Why this role is materially affected**

The Sales team spends almost ~40% of the time (based on people interviews and quoted stats) in administrative activities associated with searching for information linked to product recommendations or customer-linked information that is required to either answer customer queries or create a customer value proposition.

**Current value bottleneck in the role**

Fragmented information sitting across Sharepoint libraries that tend to go stale due to the absence of any structured information governance and might not be completely applicable / relevant during the investigation and ultimately, leading to lengthy verification by a human entity before any kind of an analysis or proposal can be drafted.

**What should not be assumed**

Automation or redesign based on AI interventions is expected to reduce the time spent on non-administrative tasks allowing the Sales Manager to spend more time in pursuing sales related insights like account planning, account strategy reviews, customer engagements etc. rather than focus on information-retrieval and similar administrative tasks. This initiative is not meant to reduce head-count but, expected to improve the productivity of the sales manager community through elimination or reduction of administrative tasks specific to customer query and product recommendations. 

**Recommended collection aid - evidence basis**

The shared evidence is based on leading employee interviews with the sales manager community and is being shared basis these conversations.

**Rubric coverage check:** role-opportunity link; material activity impact; current value bottleneck; activities/value rather than headcount reduction.

**Classification guide - worksheet and course notes**

| Classification | Test to apply |
| --- | --- |
| Automatable | Clear inputs, rules and expected output; reliable machine execution; the organisation is comfortable without human review |

| Irreducibly human | Human judgement, broader context, accountability or relationships are essential |
