**What is the single most important role-design change required so that people experience AI as capability expansion rather than role erosion?**
Make AI the account manager's research assistant, not the decision-maker [J]. AI identifies, organises and synthesises qualitative, quantitative and image-based evidence; the manager owns framing the problem, investigating, gathering the missing context and making the trade-offs behind the final recommendation [J].
