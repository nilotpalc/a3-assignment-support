### B4. Cross-functional compound-value check

**Selected opportunity:** Sector-matched value cases (Priority 1) [L:P4=Quick Win] [J].

**Upstream function / AI output it depends on:** Product and technical teams supply well-defined technical literature, customer success stories, equipment-specific recommendation guidelines and product performance analysis, including entry, mainstream and premium tier positioning and the machine-performance reasons to move up a tier [J]. The AI-tagged evidence library built from this content is the upstream output the assistant relies on [J].

**Downstream function / decision it affects:** Sales teams' choice of the right lubricant for a customer's equipment, and their wallet-expansion offers [J].

**How value could compound:** Once reliable, the conversational assistant could evolve into a customer self-service offer for routine queries, freeing scarce technical experts for complex problem-solving and for curating the knowledge that improves the assistant further [J].

**How error or bias could propagate:** If the knowledge repository is not version-controlled, the assistant may recommend withdrawn, discontinued or superseded products [J]. That error passes straight into customer conversations, risking brand erosion and, at worst, lost tenders or product-performance liabilities [J].

**Control / hand-off needed:** Product or usage recommendations returned with low confidence are escalated to a subject-matter expert before reaching the customer [J]. This depends on named content owners keeping the repository versioned, the readiness condition set in B3 [J].
