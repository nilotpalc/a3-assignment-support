**Selected opportunity**
P4, sector-matched value cases, our Priority 1 [L:P4=Quick Win].

**Role / role family affected**
Sales Account Manager [J].

**Why this role is materially affected**
Interviews with sales managers suggest that about 40% of their time goes on administrative work [A:AS2]. Most of it is searching for product and customer information needed to answer customer queries or build a value proposition [J]. P4 targets exactly this retrieval and drafting effort [J].

**Current value bottleneck in the role**
Relevant content is spread across document libraries with no structured ownership, so it goes stale and may not fit the case in hand [J]. Each answer or proposal therefore needs lengthy human checking before analysis can begin [J].

**What should not be assumed**
P4 is not a headcount-reduction initiative [J]. The aim is to cut administrative retrieval and checking so that account managers spend more time on account planning, account strategy reviews and customer engagement [J]. Any time released is an estimate until it is measured (see D5) [A:AS2].
