Proposed plan only: no trial has been run, and no output reaches a customer before human review [J]. Ten anonymised or synthetic enquiries per condition, matched for difficulty and rotated across conditions; one SME scores outputs blind to condition [J].

| Condition | What will be measured | Expected learning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human alone | SLA time for data collation, proposal build, decision and end to end [J] | Baseline performance [J] |
| AI alone | Same SLA times, plus accuracy, wrong-source count and SME recommendation rating (0-5) [J] | Where AI fails without human context [J] |
| Human + AI | Same measures, plus review, correction and escalation effort [J] | Whether collaboration beats both, and at what review cost [J] |

**Primary metric(s):** Average end-to-end SLA time; accuracy and explainability rated 0-5 by the SME, target at least 4/5 [J].

**Task(s) likely inside AI capability frontier:** D2 tasks 1, 4, 5, 9, 11 and 14, and task 7 for retrieval only: research, data structuring, retrieval and clause extraction [J].

**Task(s) where human judgement/context remains important:** Tasks 6 and 17 (external conditions versus performance); 10, 13, 15 and 16 (equipment-specific expert guidance); 7 (whether OEM guidance applies); 2 and 19 (customer preferences) [J]. Task 7 sits on both sides, illustrating the jagged frontier [C:M3] [J].

**What result would justify the redesigned collaboration pattern:** Human + AI cuts average end-to-end SLA by 20-40% against the baseline with a rating of at least 4/5, keeping AI as the account manager's research assistant [J].

**What result would make you redesign the workflow again:** SLA reduction below 20% with a rating below 3/5 [J]. Ratings between 3 and 4, or mixed results such as strong SLA gains with a rating below 4, are inconclusive: extend the trial with more cases before deciding [J].
