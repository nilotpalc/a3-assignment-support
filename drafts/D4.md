**D4.1 Role family:** AI Product Managers [J].

**Why this role/capability is the current bottleneck:** The P4 assistant must fit how account managers build value cases today, with SME review and approval points built into the experience [J]. Someone must translate that workflow into AI scope across retrieval-based GenAI synthesis and deep-learning image and video processing [J]. Supporting need: AI Systems Engineers to integrate a prototype embedding approval and governance steps [J].

**Build internally / hire / source externally - and why:** Build internally: existing lubricant-solution product owners gain AI product credentials, and internal AI Systems Engineers are allocated [J]. Hiring would delay a Quick Win; internal owners know the content and can run the SME feedback that keeps it current [J].

**D4.2 Framework selected:** Accenture Transition-Stage [J].

**Why this framework fits the maturity evidence from Part A and what one structural/organisational change it would prioritise:** Talent and Process are Low [R:Talent=Low] [R:Process=Low], yet the function is pushing from GenAI experiments to agentic AI [J]. Staged capability-building suits this better than sociotechnical redesign or principle-led structure, which assume absent maturity [C:M3] [J]. Priority change: hub-and-spoke, with certified product owners embedded in Sales as AI product managers, drawing standards, engineers and reusable components from the group AI CoE so central expertise does not become a silo [J].
