### B3. Sequence the portfolio and select the top three

Sequencing logic: impact weighed against readiness and capability [J].

| Priority | Opportunity | Why this should move now / next | Readiness condition or dependency | What would change the priority |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sector-matched value cases [L:P4=Quick Win] | Visible, frequent use across sales teams; the smallest data-readiness gap; source content changes rarely, so little immediate governance effort is needed [J]. | A version-controlled knowledge repository with named content owners [J]. | Existing documentation proves poor or incomplete; or a full product-portfolio revamp makes the content obsolete [J]. |
| 2 | Explainable lead-generation research [L:P3=Strategic Bet] | The main gap, governance, can be agreed within a quarter [J]. It follows value cases because batches run less often, so it builds trust in AI more slowly [J]. | Prompts, taxonomy and scoring rules under version control, owned by a named Marketing Intelligence product owner [J]. | Prompt designs vary so much by industry sector that standardisation fails, leaving ad hoc use rather than a governed platform [J]. |
| 3 | Order-status / lead-time exception visibility [L:P2=Strategic Bet] | Cuts the order-update fire-fighting that absorbs sales and customer-operations time, freeing it for revenue conversations [J]. | A cleaned, integrated delivery-level dataset with logistics-network attributes, covering six months per target market [J]. | If order, despatch and credit-block timestamps cannot be joined into one record, the prediction layer fails and the use case reverts to a reporting view rather than AI [J]. |

**Balance:** Value cases deliver early, visible impact and build confidence in AI [J]. Lead generation and order status are capability builds that create the process standards and owned data definitions identified as the two binding constraints in A4 [J]. Churn early warning and the LT evidence pack are held back as options, not dropped: they reuse these foundations (governed data products; web-search tooling and process governance) once their triggers are met [J].
