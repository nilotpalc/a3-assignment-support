**Frequency basis.** The frequency categories (High = every customer interaction; Medium = interactions needing a lubrication investigation, a proposal or follow-up; Low = complex investigation scenarios only) are the author's estimates from interviews with sales managers, the same basis as D1 [A:AS3]. They are event-based, not time-and-motion data, and do not represent shares of total role time [A:AS3].

**Evidence and coverage.** The 20 tasks cover the Sales Account Manager workflow from pre-engagement research to trial close-out [J]. The author reviewed and revised the classifications, including task 6 to Irreducibly human and task 12 to Augmentable [J]. No task is classified Automatable, because no step is yet acceptable without human review [J]. Tasks 2, 19 and 20 are kept whole because each has one accountable output: an agreed visit scope, a released recommendation and a validated trial outcome [J].
